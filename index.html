Privacy policy — Sprint Capacity Ledger for Jira

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Runs on Atlassian

Sprint Capacity Ledger is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure and stores its data inside Atlassian Forge app storage. The app does not operate any server of its own and does not send data to any third party.

What the app stores

DataWhereWhy
Sprint plan settings (hours per story point, default hours/day, working days, sprint dates, holidays) Forge app storage, keyed per board and sprintTo render the capacity calculation
Team roster (Atlassian account ID, display name, hours per day, active flag, per-day hour overrides) Forge app storage, keyed per board and sprintTo compute daily and sprint capacity
Leave records (Atlassian account ID, display name, date range, leave type, optional hours, optional reason, who added it and when) Forge app storage, keyed per boardTo subtract absence from capacity
Board and project defaults (default project choice) Forge app storageTo remember the user's setup

Nothing else is collected. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no profiling.

What the app reads from Jira

Read-only, at the moment a page is opened: boards, sprints, sprint dates, the project list, and the users assignable to a project (for the team picker). The app never reads or writes issue content, never transitions issues, and holds only read:* scopes plus storage:app.

Data sharing

None. The app makes no outbound network requests, which is why it qualifies for the Runs on Atlassian programme. Atlassian hosts both the compute and the storage. There are no sub-processors.

Retention and deletion

  • Data lives as long as the app is installed.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its Forge storage, removing all plans, rosters and leave records.
  • Individual records can be deleted in the app at any time: leave records via Remove, people via Manage team → Remove, day overrides via Clear.

Data location

Wherever the customer's Atlassian cloud data is hosted, governed by Atlassian's data-residency commitments. The app stores no copies elsewhere.

Personal data

The only personal data processed is what Jira already holds — Atlassian account ID and display name — plus the working-hours and absence information a user deliberately enters. Legal basis is performance of the customer's contract for the app.
